Formulas and assumptions

Per unit. Arc minutes = weld length ÷ travel speed. Labor and overhead use arc time + non-arc handling time + gas pre/postflow time. Wire consumed = feed speed × arc minutes × wire weight per inch. For SMAW, purchased electrode = melt-off rate × arc minutes × weight per electrode ÷ usable electrode fraction. Multiply purchased filler weight by price per pound. Gas uses arc and pre/postflow time. Flux uses the entered pounds consumed per unit.

Per foot. Arc minutes per foot = 12 ÷ travel speed. Labor and overhead use arc minutes ÷ operator factor. Wire, stick, and gas costs use arc minutes per foot. Flux cost uses weld weight per foot × flux ratio × flux price.

Per pound deposited. Labor and overhead = hourly rate ÷ (deposition rate × operator factor). Filler cost = filler price ÷ process efficiency. Gas cost = flow × gas price ÷ deposition rate. Flux cost = flux ratio × flux price.

Operator factor, process efficiency, and usable electrode percentage are entered as percentages. Gas price is per cubic foot. Flux ratio is pounds of flux consumed per pound deposited. Set unused consumables to zero. Other costs can include additional consumables or base material on the selected cost basis.
