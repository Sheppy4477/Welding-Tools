Each calculator works independently. Choose the variable to solve for, then enter the other known values. Results update automatically. Use latest geometry result to import weld area and density from Geometry & weight in this browser, then optionally copy the weight into the travel calculator.

Formulas & input units

Weld weight per foot: Ww = weld geometry area (in²) × 12 in × material density (lb/in³). Result is in lb/ft. Imported area includes reinforcement and both fillets when applicable. Inputs remain editable after import.

Deposition rate: Dr = 13.1 × (wire diameter)² × wire feed speed × process efficiency. Diameter is in inches, feed is in in/min, and Dr is in lb/hr. Efficiency is converted from a percentage to a decimal (95% = 0.95).

Travel speed: Ts = (Dr × number of weld passes) ÷ (5 × weld metal weight per foot). Weight is entered in lb/ft; travel speed is displayed in in/min. Weld metal weight per foot must be greater than zero.

Operator factor: Of = (arc time ÷ total time) × 100. Enter both times in minutes. Total time must be greater than zero and arc time cannot exceed total time.
