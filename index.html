Weld estimate

Planning estimate only. Confirm geometry, consumable data, and actual production conditions. These calculations do not qualify welding procedures.

Calculation methods & assumptions

All geometry and stock dimensions describe one finished unit. Weld length includes all joints/sides on that unit. Quantity multiplies the finished-unit totals.

Weld area: fillet = leg A × leg B ÷ 2; single V groove = root gap × depth + depth² × tan(included angle ÷ 2); custom = entered cross-sectional area. The V groove assumes a full-depth bevel without a root face. Extra weld metal adds a percentage to area for reinforcement or overfill. Pass count does not multiply final weld volume.

Weight: weld area × total weld length × weld density. Stock volume × stock density × pieces per unit. Stock volume is gross purchased geometry; joint preparation and cutouts are not subtracted. Round tube uses outside diameter and wall thickness.

Deposition: solid wire area × wire feed IPM × filler density × efficiency = lb/min. Multiply by 60 for lb/hr. For cored wire, stick, or other processes, use the direct deposition-rate mode with supplier or measured data. Efficiency also determines purchased filler weight.

Time and travel: arc minutes = weld lb ÷ deposited lb/min. Effective fill travel IPM = total weld length ÷ arc minutes; for multipass work this is a joint-completion rate, not individual bead travel speed. Measured mode uses bead travel IPM and passes to calculate arc minutes and the required deposition rate. Operator factor = arc-on minutes ÷ total working minutes × 100. Paid production minutes = arc minutes ÷ operator factor; separate setup minutes are added afterward.

Cost: stock lb × stock $/lb + purchased filler lb × filler $/lb + paid hours × (labor + overhead $/hr) + arc hours × arc consumables $/hr + setup hours × (labor + overhead). Consumables should include gas, power, and other arc-time costs as needed. Cost/lb deposited excludes stock purchase cost; finished cost/lb includes stock and weld weight. No markup, tax, freight, or scrap allowance is included.

Reference: TWI: Welding costs · Lincoln Electric: wire feed and electrode efficiency
