Ideal geometric fill; fused parent metal at a land is not counted again as added weld metal. Cap and root reinforcement use a crown approximation: area = (width × height) ÷ 2. Formulas & geometry conventions

For all profiles, gross material area = 2 × plate width × thickness. Net material area = gross area − preparation cutout area. Net volume = net area × plate/weld length. Material weight = net volume × material density. Fillets remove no material.

Fillet area = leg A × leg B / 2. Symmetric V area = root gap × thickness + (thickness − land)² × tan(included angle / 2). Two equal plates are used; plate length equals weld length, material and weld densities can be overridden separately, and net plate area subtracts both bevel triangles. Root reinforcement width equals root gap. Single bevel area = gap × depth + (depth − land)² × tan(bevel angle) / 2. With no land, land = 0. The root gap is filled through the full depth; the land itself remains parent material.

U groove: two equal circular root corners of radius R meet straight walls tangent to the circles. Let θ = included angle / 2, H = depth − land, y₀ = R(1 − sin θ), x₀ = R cos θ. Each curved side has x(y) = √[R² − (y − R)²] for 0 ≤ y ≤ y₀. Above y₀, x(y) = x₀ + (y − y₀)tan θ. The removed area is twice the integral of x(y) from 0 to H. The radius must fit completely within H. Weld area = removed area + gap × depth.

Weld volume = (ideal area + cap area + root area) × length. Each reinforcement area = (width × height) ÷ 2. Cap width is the groove top opening; fillets have no reinforcement. Net material volume = gross material volume − groove cutout volume for all groove profiles. Combined weight = net material weight + deposited weld weight. Reinforcement does not increase the groove cutout.

The U groove profile exposes radius, root face / land, root gap, and included angle, as illustrated in Rolled Alloys fabrication guidance. This calculator uses ideal geometry, not a procedure qualification or a prescribed joint design.
