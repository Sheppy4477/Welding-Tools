Workbook formulas and cooling rules

Net heat input H = voltage × amperage × 60 ÷ (travel speed × 1,000) × arc efficiency in kJ/in. Multiply by 0.947817 for BTU/in. The formulas below use H in BTU/in, ΔT = Tc − To, and k = thermal conductivity ÷ 3,600 ÷ 12.

Relative plate thickness = b × √(density × specific heat × ΔT ÷ H). Use thin only at 0.6 or less, both between 0.6 and 0.9, and thick only at 0.9 or greater. Both calculated values are shown with the applicable rule. Cooling rates retain the workbook’s signs and −°F/s label.

Thin cooling = −2π × density × specific heat × ΔT³ × (b/H)² × k. Thick cooling = −2π × ΔT² × k ÷ H.

Peak temperature = To + H(Tm − To) ÷ [density × specific heat × b × y × √(2πe) × (Tm − To) + H]. Solidification rate = latent heat × H × density ÷ [k × 2π × density × specific heat × (Tm − To)²], in seconds.

Plate thickness and distance use inches. In the formulas above, ρ is density, C is specific heat, L is latent heat of fusion, and Tm is melting temperature. The conductivity in the reference table is converted to k by dividing by 3,600 and then by 12.
